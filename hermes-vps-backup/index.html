Hermes VPS Backup

Hermes VPS Backup is an independently operated utility for recovery copies of its operator’s own Hermes Agent installation. It stores encrypted backup files in the operator’s Google Drive account.

This is a private utility, separate from the Pryara EOI service described elsewhere on this domain. It offers no public registration and does not ask website visitors to connect their Google accounts.

What the utility does

The utility uses restic to encrypt recovery copies before upload and rclone to connect to Google Drive. The recovery password is held separately from the Google authorization credentials.

Recovery copies can contain application configuration, conversation history, memory, approved workspace files and credentials needed to restore the operator’s installation. These files are encrypted before being stored in Google Drive.

Google Drive access

The connection requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permission allows it to create and manage files associated with the app’s backup connection. It does not request unrestricted access to all Drive files, Gmail or Calendar.

Google receives encrypted repository files and the metadata needed to store and transfer them. Encryption does not conceal the account identity, storage usage or transfer metadata from Google.

Current backup status

Encrypted backup and recovery checks have been completed. Automatic nightly backups are not yet enabled. Unattended scheduling will be activated only after durable authorization and scheduling checks have passed.

The intended scheduled retention is seven daily and four weekly recovery points, which can overlap. That policy is not yet running automatically. The privacy notice explains retained recovery copies and deletion limits.

Operator and privacy contact

The utility is operated privately by the account holder who authorizes the backup connection. It is not operated as part of Pryara’s customer EOI service.

For privacy questions about this utility, email privacy@pryara.com and identify Hermes VPS Backup in your message.
