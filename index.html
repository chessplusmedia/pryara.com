Why evidence comes first

Under the World Bank’s fraud and corruption rules, a misrepresentation in an EOI made knowingly or recklessly is a fraudulent practice, and firms can be sanctioned for it. So we never fill a gap with a guess. Every statement about your firm is traced to your own document or written answer, and anything we cannot trace is marked for you to confirm or remove.

How it works

  1. Send the REOI. We check it is still open, not held for another firm, and that at least 7 working days remain.
  2. Readiness check (free, no files yet). For each criterion, you tell us which assignments you would cite and what proof exists. If your evidence will not support a credible EOI, we tell you why and do not quote.
  3. Quote and hold. The quote reserves the REOI for you for 24 hours. One firm per REOI, always.
  4. Pay = booked. USD 150, paid in advance by card (USD) or by card or M-Pesa (KES), with an eTIMS invoice.
  5. Delivery at least 3 working days before the REOI deadline. You check, sign and submit. We never contact the procuring entity for you.

Example. An REOI closes on Friday 20 November. Payment must arrive by Wednesday 11 November (7 working days before). The pack reaches you by Tuesday 17 November at the latest, leaving three working days to check, sign and submit.
