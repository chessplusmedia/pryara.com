SYNTHETIC SAMPLE. Not a real company, client or submission. The firm “Mto Safi Environmental & Social Consultants Ltd”, its staff figures, assignments and amounts are invented. Public bodies and the REOI are described in general terms only. Produced in Pryara’s internal practice run (October 2026) with the same tools, steps and founder review as a paid order, then shortened for this page.

The REOI (generic): a World Bank-financed Request for Expressions of Interest for consulting services, firm selection by Consultant’s Qualification Selection (CQS): an Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (ESIA) and a Resettlement Action Plan (RAP) for two clusters of a feeder-roads programme in a West African country.

A paid pack has three files, all in Word: the criteria checklist, the claim → evidence table and the EOI. Below are excerpts of each (assignments A2 and A3 are not shown).
